Fidel Tito

M.S. Computer Vision · University of Central Florida

Fulbright Scholar · Multimodal LLMs and medical image understanding

I'm a Fulbright Scholar and M.S. student in Computer Vision at the University of Central Florida. I have worked with Dr. Yogesh Rawat and Dr. Chen Chen at UCF's Center for Research in Computer Vision (CRCV) and Institute of Artificial Intelligence (IAI). My current research is on multimodal large language models, in two directions: how they reason when several agents each see part of a scene, and medical image understanding, where I'm exploring visual question answering on kidney CT scans.

Before graduate school I spent four years in industry building production ML and LLM systems, from MLOps platforms to text-to-SQL and text-to-code assistants. In summer 2026 I was an ML Engineer Intern at Adobe, working on recommendations for the Acrobat AI Assistant. I studied Mechatronics Engineering at Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería in Lima, Peru.

  2. BMVC 2026Oral

    SignRR: Retrieve and Refine Real Motion for Sign Language Production

    F. Tito, A. Sanchez, S. Farfan, G. Bejarano

    Builds sign language motion out of real signing: it retrieves matching segments from a gloss-motion dictionary, then refines the whole sequence with a part-aware residual VQ-VAE. State-of-the-art back-translation among methods that don't use ground truth, on PHOENIX14T and CSL-Daily.

  3. MICCAI 2026 Workshop (AMAI)Oral

    Longitudinal 3D Foundation Modeling for Neoadjuvant Breast Cancer Response Prediction from Serial DCE-MRI

    F. Tito, N. Ghafouri, Z. Wang, P. Khosravi, Y. Tian, C. Chen

    Encodes four DCE-MRI scans per patient with a frozen 3D foundation model, models how they change during treatment with a temporal transformer, and fuses clinical data to predict pathologic complete response. Evaluated on 982 patients from I-SPY2 and ACRIN-6698.
